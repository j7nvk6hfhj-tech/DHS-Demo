Help guide

DHS Logistics Order Dashboard
Open the dashboard

New here? Start with this

The dashboard keeps every cement order in one place, gives each order a token, and lines orders up for dispatch, oldest first. Your designation decides what you can do (see Who can do what):

  • Dispatch: read Signing in, Taking a new order, Dispatching an order, Changing or cancelling and Vehicles and tracking.
  • Supervisor: the same, plus Dealer list.
  • Admin: everything, plus Team and emails and Summary and Excel.
  • Viewer: Finding your way and Finding orders.

Stuck on something? Type a word in the search box at the top.

Signing in

  1. Your Admin must first add your email to the dashboard. Until then, you can't get in.
  2. Gmail: tap Sign in with Google and pick your account.
  3. Any other email, first time: type your email and a new password (at least 6 characters), then tap Create account. Open the link we email you, come back and tap I've verified. From then on, use Sign in.
  4. Forgot your password? Type your email, then tap Forgot password? to get a reset link.

If you see "not on the authorised list", the Admin has to add the exact email you signed in with. Check the spelling, and check you didn't pick a different Gmail account.

One device per account

Each person's account works on one phone or computer at a time. Signing in on a new device signs the old one out, with a message saying so. Keep your password to yourself; sharing it means you keep signing each other out, and the Admin can see which device each account is on.

On an iPhone, the installed app and Safari count as two devices, so use the installed app.

Put it on your phone

  • Android (Chrome): tap Install app at the top of the dashboard. If nothing happens, open the browser menu ⋮ and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone: open the dashboard in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Computer: in Chrome or Edge, tap Install app.

It then opens from its own icon, like any app. You still sign in the same way.

At the top

  • The red number on Orders is how many orders are waiting.
  • Your name (top right) opens a menu with Help, the language switch and Sign out.
  • Help opens this guide at the part about the page you're on.
  • हिंदी switches tab names, headings, column names and field labels to Hindi; English switches back. Orders, names, products and buttons stay as typed, in English. Each phone or computer remembers its own choice.
  • Live (green) means everything is up to date. Reconnect means the connection dropped: reload the page.

Taking a new order

  1. Order date & time is set to the moment you save, which keeps the queue fair. Only the Admin can enter a different time, for example a call at 10:15 that's entered at 10:40; ask the Admin if that's needed.
  2. Dealer: type a few letters and pick from the list. Every order needs a dealer. Not in the list? Ask the Admin or a Supervisor to add it under Settings → Dealers.
  3. Party / customer: who the cement is for. Any name.
  4. Phone and Delivery location are optional, but they help the driver.
  5. Products & bags: pick the product and type the number of 50 kg bags. For a mixed order, tap + Add product (up to 5). The total shows underneath.
  6. Vehicle no. and Driver are optional. You can add them later with Edit.
  7. Tap Add order & get token. A ticket shows the token and the order's place in the dispatch queue. The form clears for the next order.
  8. To let the customer know, tap Send token on WhatsApp on the ticket. WhatsApp opens with the message ready; check it and tap Send. With no phone number on the order, WhatsApp asks you to pick the contact.

If the same dealer already has an order for the same party (or the same phone number) in the last 12 hours, the dashboard shows "Looks like a repeat of token …". Check it's really a new order, then tap Save anyway. Changing any field clears the warning.

Token and "in line"

  • Token is the order's number for its day. The earliest order of the day is token 1. It's on the yellow ticket with the date. Give this number to the customer.
  • In line is the order's place in the dispatch queue, counting all days. Number 1 goes next. It goes down as the orders ahead are dispatched.

A token changes only if someone adds an order with an earlier time on the same day, or deletes an order. To keep numbers steady, cancel an order instead of deleting it.

Dispatching an order Admin · Supervisor · Dispatch

  1. Open Orders → Queue. The oldest pending order is at the top. The red Waiting time shows how long it has waited.
  2. When the truck leaves, tap Mark dispatched. The time is filled in as now; change it if the truck left earlier.
  3. Tap Confirm dispatched. The order leaves the queue, and the time taken is recorded.

After dispatching, a green note offers Send update on WhatsApp: it tells the customer the dispatch time, vehicle and driver. Tap Hide to close the note.

Marked the wrong one? Open that order (tap it in All orders or Today's list) and tap Undo dispatch. It goes back to its place in the queue.

Each card shows Entered by: who took the order. If there's any confusion about an order, ask that person first.

Correcting a dispatch time later (in the order window) is for a Supervisor or the Admin. Viewers see the Mark dispatched button greyed out.

Track on a card opens the truck's live location in WheelsEye. It shows when the order's vehicle number has a link under Settings → Vehicles.

Changing, cancelling or deleting an order

  • Change: tap Edit on a queue card, or tap the order in Today's list or All orders. Fix what's wrong and tap Save changes.
  • Cancel (customer called it off): open the order, set Mark as to Cancelled, then Save changes. It leaves the queue but stays in the records and Excel, with its token. To bring it back, set Mark as to Pending.
  • Delete Admin (only for duplicates or test entries): tap Delete order, then tap again to confirm. It's gone for good, and later tokens that day move up by one. Others don't see this button; they cancel instead.
  • Who changed it? Under the order's heading you'll see who entered it and, after any change, Last changed … by ….
  • The order window also has a WhatsApp button to send the token, or the dispatch update once it's dispatched.

Who can change what: the Admin can change any order, including its time and orders already dispatched. A Supervisor can change or cancel any order that isn't dispatched yet. Dispatch can change or cancel only the orders they entered themselves, on the same day, before dispatch. Viewers can't open orders. If you can't change an order, the order window tells you why; ask a Supervisor or the Admin.

Finding orders (All orders)

  • Today's orders, and any day that still has pending orders, show straight away. The Entered by column, next to the order time, shows who took each order.
  • Use Dealer, Status and Search to narrow the list. Search finds the party, the location or the phone number (type at least 3 digits).
  • Older dates: set From and To. A yellow bar says those dates aren't loaded yet; tap Load these dates. Old orders load only when someone asks, which keeps the dashboard free to run.
  • Download Excel saves the orders on screen.

Summary and Excel

  • Pick a Month. The cards at the top show orders, pending, dispatched, bags and average time from order to dispatch.
  • Dealer-wise and Day-wise tables: tap a row, choose the dates, and tap Download to get that dealer's or day's orders as Excel.
  • Refresh updates the totals of older days.
  • Admin only: Download master file gives the whole month in one Excel. Recalculate month fixes the totals if they ever look wrong.

Only the Admin's Excel files (and the monthly master file email) include the time taken columns, per order and the average. Everyone else's files, and the weekly report, leave them out.

On Android, Excel files go to Downloads. On iPhone, look in the Files app under Downloads.

Vehicles and tracking Admin · Supervisor · Dispatch

  1. In the WheelsEye app, select the truck, tap Share location and copy the link.
  2. In Settings → Vehicles, type the truck number exactly as you write it on orders, add the driver, paste the link and tap Save vehicle.
  3. Every order with that vehicle number now shows a Track button.

WheelsEye links stop working after about 7 days. The Link status column shows the days left. When it says Expired, or orders show Track (old link), tap New link and paste a fresh one.

The Admin, Supervisors and Dispatch can add vehicles and paste new links; only the Admin can remove a vehicle. Viewers don't see this page.

Anyone with a link can see that truck, so keep links inside the team.

Dealer list

Settings → Dealers shows the authorised dealers. These names fill the Dealer list on every order.

The Admin or a Supervisor can add a dealer; only the Admin can remove one. Removing a dealer doesn't change old orders.

Team and emails Admin

  • Add a person: in Settings → Team & emails, type their name and email, pick a role and tap Add person. They can sign in straight away.
  • Change a role any time. It applies immediately. Remove people who leave on their last day.
  • Device shows which phone or computer each person is signed in on, and since when. If a phone is lost, or an account seems to be shared, tap Sign out device (tap twice). They can then sign in again on one device.
  • Report emails: the Daily contact list (dealer, party, phone and location of every order since the 1st of the month; starts afresh each month), the Weekly report and the Monthly master file each have an on/off switch, a time and their own list of recipients. Emails usually arrive 5 to 10 minutes after the set time.

When the internet is slow or off

  • The dashboard keeps showing what it already loaded.
  • Orders and changes you make while offline are kept on this phone or computer and saved once the internet is back. Keep the dashboard open until then.
  • If the top shows Reconnect or a message about the connection, reload the page.
  • Loading older dates, and Excel files from the Summary, need the internet. This guide works without it.

Quick answers

I typed something wrong in an order
Open the order (Edit on the queue card, or tap it in a list), fix it and tap Save changes. See Changing an order.
It says "Looks like a repeat of token …"
The same dealer already has an order for that party (or that phone number) in the last 12 hours. If it's the same order, don't save it again. If the customer really ordered again, tap Save anyway.
How do I send the token to the customer?
Tap Send token on WhatsApp on the ticket after saving, or open the order and tap WhatsApp token. After dispatch, use Send update on WhatsApp.
I can't find the Delete button
Only the Admin can delete orders. Cancel it instead: open the order, set Mark as to Cancelled and save.
I can't change an order
The order window says why. Usually it's already dispatched (only the Admin can change it then), or, for Dispatch, it was entered by someone else or on an earlier day. Ask a Supervisor or the Admin.
Why can't I change the order time?
The order time is set when you save, so the queue stays fair. Only the Admin can enter or change an order's time.
Who changed this order?
Open the order. Under its heading you'll see who entered it and who last changed it, with the time. The Excel files show the same.
The customer cancelled
Open the order, set Mark as to Cancelled and save. Don't delete it; cancelled orders stay in the records.
I marked the wrong order as dispatched
Open that order and tap Undo dispatch. The Admin, a Supervisor or Dispatch can do this.
The Mark dispatched button is grey
Viewers can't mark orders dispatched. If you should be able to, ask the Admin to change your designation.
The dealer isn't in the list
Only the Admin can add dealers, under Settings → Dealers. Once added, it appears in the list on every phone straight away.
A token number changed
Someone added an order with an earlier time on that day, or deleted an order. See Token and "in line".
I can't find an older order
Go to Orders → All orders, set the From and To dates, and tap Load these dates.
Track says "old link", or the map doesn't open
The WheelsEye link has expired. The Admin, a Supervisor or Dispatch copies a fresh link from WheelsEye and taps New link under Settings → Vehicles.
I can't see Team & emails
That page is only for the Admin.
It says I'm "not on the authorised list"
Ask the Admin to add the exact email you signed in with. Then tap Sign out and sign in again.
The verification or password email didn't come
Check the spam or promotions folder. On the sign-in screen, tap Send link again.
The Excel file didn't download
Check the internet and try again. On Android, look in Downloads; on iPhone, in the Files app.
The weekly or monthly email didn't arrive
Check spam first. The Admin can check the recipients, the switch and the last check under Settings → Team & emails.
Install app does nothing on my iPhone
On iPhone, open the dashboard in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Where is Sign out?
Tap your name at the top right of the dashboard. Sign out is at the bottom of that menu.
I was signed out: "signed in on another device"
Your account was just signed in on a different phone or computer, and an account works on one device at a time. If that was you, carry on there, or sign in again here to switch back. If it wasn't you, tell your Admin straight away and change your password.
Who took this order?
Queue cards show Entered by, and so does the column next to the order time in All orders. Today's list on New Order shows the name too.
A message says "Your access changed" or "Lost the live connection"
Reload the page. If it keeps happening, tell the Admin.

Still stuck? Reach your Admin

Your Admin is the person who added you to the dashboard. Tell them which page you were on, what you tapped, and what you saw. A screenshot helps.
