Vehicles & GPS tracking

Paste a truck's WheelsEye link; orders with that vehicle number then show Track.

How to get a tracking link

  1. Open the WheelsEye app and select the truck.
  2. Tap Share location and copy the link (it starts with https://wheelseye.com/ui/track).
  3. Paste it in the form above; the truck number fills in. Tap Save vehicle.

Anyone who has a tracking link can see that truck's location, so only share it inside the team.

Team & roles

Only emails listed here can sign in. Role changes apply immediately.

Who can do what

AdminEverything, including this Team & emails page, changing order times, changing dispatched orders, deleting orders, renaming and removing dealers, removing vehicles, and the master file.
SupervisorAdd, change and cancel any order that isn't dispatched; hold, reschedule, mark dispatched, correct dispatch times and undo; add dealers and edit their contact details; add vehicles and tracking links.
DispatchAdd orders; change only their own orders from today; cancel, hold or reschedule any order that isn't dispatched; mark dispatched and undo; edit dealers' contact details; add vehicles and tracking links.
ViewerSees the queue, orders and summaries and can download Excel. Can't open order details, see vehicles, or change anything.

These limits are enforced by the database security rules, not just hidden on screen.
