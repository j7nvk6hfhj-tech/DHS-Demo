Vehicles & GPS tracking

Paste each truck's WheelsEye tracking link here. Orders with that vehicle number then show a Track button. WheelsEye links appear to expire after 7 days, so paste a fresh one when a link shows as expired.

How to get a tracking link

  1. Open the WheelsEye app and select the truck.
  2. Tap Share location and copy the link (it starts with https://wheelseye.com/ui/track).
  3. Paste it below with the truck number and tap Save.

Anyone who has a tracking link can see that truck's location, so only share it inside the team.

Team & roles

Only emails listed here can sign in. Change a person's role any time; it applies immediately.

Who can do what

AdminEverything, including this Team & emails page, changing order times, changing dispatched orders, deleting orders, removing dealers and vehicles, and the master file.
SupervisorAdd, change and cancel any order that isn't dispatched yet; mark dispatched, correct dispatch times and undo; add dealers; add vehicles and tracking links.
DispatchAdd orders; change or cancel only their own orders from today; mark dispatched and undo; add vehicles and tracking links.
ViewerSees the queue, orders and summaries and can download Excel. Can't open order details, see vehicles, or change anything.

These limits are enforced by the database security rules, not just hidden on screen.
